RaaS & Blockchain

How RaaS Works in Our Ecosystem

Our system transforms physical robotics into a distributed service layer. Builders manufacture robotic arms from our open-source designs, connect them using our software, and register as RaaS nodes by staking tokens. These nodes become globally available for on-demand use — whether for testing, research, or industrial tasks. Users pay tokens for access, operators earn from uptime, and the protocol automatically redistributes fees across the network. It’s robotics infrastructure — owned by the community.
